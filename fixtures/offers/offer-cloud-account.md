# Cloud account

## What it is

An account with no monthly fee, as published in the bank's fee documents (cloud-fees-2026 and the fee document of each product).

## Terms

Monthly fees as published:

- Cloud €0
- Horizon €3
- Travel €4
- Family €5
- Aurora €6 (€0 only with the salary and card conditions of that account)
- Professional €9

## What it is not

The documentation does not say whether or how a product can be changed, so this is not an offer to switch. Only the fees are compared here: the other conditions of the accounts may differ.
