# Salary Plan

## What it is

A free set of services for customers whose salary is paid into their account here.

## Terms

- Free.
- For a salary of at least €600 paid into the account two months in a row.
- A notice on the day the salary arrives.
- A standing transfer to one of your own accounts on salary day, set up with a manager.
- Support cases answered the next working day.

## What it is not

It does not change the monthly fee of your account and it pays no cash bonus. It is not a referral reward.

Version 1. Valid from 2026-10-01. Demonstration content written by the project team.
