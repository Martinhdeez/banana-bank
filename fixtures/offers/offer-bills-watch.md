# Bills & Subscriptions Watch

## What it is

One list of the payments that repeat every month, with a notice before each one is charged.

## Terms

- Free.
- Lists the recurring payments found in your movements.
- A notice 3 days before an expected charge.
- A notice when an amount changes by more than 10%.

## What it is not

It does not cancel, pause or block any payment. The list comes from your past movements, so a new or irregular payment may be missing.

Version 1. Valid from 2026-10-01. Demonstration content written by the project team.
