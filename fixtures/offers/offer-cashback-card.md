# Cashback debit card

## What it is

A debit card that gives back part of what you spend on eating out and leisure.

## Terms

- Card fee €0.
- 1% back on card purchases at restaurants, bars, cinemas and events.
- At most €8 back a month, paid the following month.
- Purchases are charged to your account balance.

## What it is not

It is a debit card: it gives no credit. Nothing is paid back on groceries, transfers or cash withdrawals.

Version 1. Valid from 2026-10-01. Demonstration content written by the project team.
