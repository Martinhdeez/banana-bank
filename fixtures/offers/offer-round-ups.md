# Round-ups

## What it is

Each card purchase is rounded up to the next euro and the difference goes to your own savings account.

## Terms

- Free.
- The differences are moved once a day.
- You can pause it at any time.
- Skipped while your main balance is under €50.

## What it is not

It needs a savings account with us: it does not open one. It pays no interest of its own and it is not a way to pay.

Version 1. Valid from 2026-10-01. Demonstration content written by the project team.
