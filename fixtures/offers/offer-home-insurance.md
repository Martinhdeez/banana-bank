# Home insurance

## What it is

Cover for the things inside a home you rent or own.

## Terms

- €9 a month. You can cancel in any month.
- Covers the contents against fire, water damage and theft, up to €20,000.
- €150 excess on each claim.
- The quote and the policy come from a manager.

## What it is not

This sheet is not a policy. It covers the contents of the home only: nothing outside it, and it is not travel insurance.
