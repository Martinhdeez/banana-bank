# Savings Pot

## What it is

A separate account for money you want to set aside, apart from the account you pay from.

## Terms

- Fee €0, no minimum balance.
- 1.25% nominal interest a year, paid monthly, on up to €50,000.
- The rate may change with 30 days' notice.
- You can move the money back at any time with an internal transfer.

## What it is not

It is not a payment account: it has no card and no direct debits. The rate is not fixed.

Version 1. Valid from 2026-10-01. Demonstration content written by the project team.
