# Aurora fee waiver

## What it is

A reminder of how the monthly fee of the Aurora account works, taken from the bank's published fee document (aurora-fees-2026).

## Terms

- The Aurora monthly fee is €6.
- It is €0 only when both of these happen in the same month: a salary payment of at least €1,200 and three settled card purchases.
- If either one is missing, the fee is €6.
- A transfer from your own account does not count as a salary payment.

## What it is not

It is not a new product and there is nothing to sign up for. It does not apply to other accounts. This app cannot see whether your purchases were made by card or whether the fee was charged, so it cannot tell you that your fee will be €0.
